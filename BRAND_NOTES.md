# TechnoDrone Brand Notes

TechnoDrone should read as a premium neon arcade flight-combat game: fast, sharp, sparse, and high-contrast.

## Name

- Use `TechnoDrone` as the project name.
- Keep the title case consistent.
- Avoid similar names that could be confused with an existing product, game, or company before public launch.

## Voice

- Concise
- Technical
- Confident
- Arcade-flavored without heavy sci-fi filler

Use `callsign` for player identity where the leaderboard needs a more in-world label.

## Visual Identity

Core tones:

- black / near-black space
- neon magenta
- electric blue
- cyan
- pale white / lilac text
- red-orange only for danger or heat

The presentation should feel engineered and luminous, not decorative.
